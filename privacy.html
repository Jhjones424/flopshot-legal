Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what the FlopShot app (“FlopShot”, “we”) collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. The short version: we collect what the app needs to keep score, sync your rounds and let you play with friends. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Using FlopShot without an account

Keeping score works without signing in. Your rounds, players and settings then stay on your phone only, and we receive none of it.

What we collect when you sign in

Account

  • From Sign in with Apple or Google: your name, your email address and an account identifier. With Apple you can choose to hide your email, and we then get a private relay address instead.
  • What you choose: your display name and your @handle, which other people use to find you.

Profile (optional)

  • Your handicap index.
  • Your Venmo username, so the people you play with can pay you.
  • Your privacy choices: who sees your rounds, and who can invite you to a round.

Golf

  • Rounds: the course and tees, the players, scores, and any putts, fairways and greens you choose to track.
  • The games you play, what each player won or lost, and the payments you mark as paid.
  • Players you add who don’t have an account (“guests”), with the name and optional Venmo username you type for them. Please only add details your friends are happy for you to add.
  • Courses you search for or add. To find a course, our server sends the search words (never your personal details) to our course-data provider.

Friends and the Feed

  • Your friends, friend requests, round invitations and the accounts you block.
  • Quips and replies you post on the Feed, and your reactions.
  • Reports you make about a quip or reply, with the reason you give and a copy of what you reported.

Support

Anything you send us when you ask for help.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or browsing activity. FlopShot has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. If you’ve allowed it in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us; those don’t identify you.

How we use it

  • To run the app: keep score, back up and sync your rounds between phones, and work out the money.
  • To show your rounds and posts to the people you choose (see below).
  • To keep the Feed safe: we block slurs and other banned words, and we review reports to remove content or suspend accounts that break the community rules.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to fix problems.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Players in a round see that round in full, including the money.
  • Your friends can see your rounds’ scores and results if you allow it (Settings › Privacy › Who sees your rounds). Friends who weren’t in a round never see its money.
  • The Feed: a round you post, and your quips and replies, are shown to friends of the people who posted that round. The Feed never shows money.
  • Your @handle and display name can be found by other signed-in users so they can add you as a friend or invite you. You choose who can invite you.
  • Your Venmo username is shown to people you play with so they can pay you.
  • Someone you block can’t see your posts, and you can’t see theirs.

Companies that help run FlopShot

These companies process data for us only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and server functions. Your account and synced data are stored with Supabase.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in. Their own privacy policies apply to your Apple or Google account.
  • GolfCourseAPI: course data. It receives course search words only.
  • Resend: sends us an email when someone reports a quip or reply, containing the reported text and the accounts involved.

When you tap a Venmo link, the Venmo app or website opens. FlopShot never sees or handles the payment, and Venmo’s own privacy policy applies.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while you have an account.
  • When you remove a round from your history, it is deleted for good after 30 days if no other account is still in it.
  • Removed Feed posts and replies are hidden at once.
  • Deleted data may stay in our provider’s backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile › Settings › Account › Delete account. This deletes at once:

  • your profile, handle, handicap index and Venmo username;
  • your friendships, invitations and blocks;
  • your Feed quips, replies and reactions;
  • rounds that nobody else with an account was in.

A round you played with other people who have accounts is their record too, so it stays for them. Your name stays on their scorecards as it was, no longer linked to any account. Reports you made, or that were made about you, are kept for safety reviews, without the reporter’s account.

Deleting the app from your phone doesn’t delete your account.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and privacy choices in Profile and Settings.
  • Download your rounds as a spreadsheet file: Settings › Your data › Export my rounds.
  • Delete a quip or reply you posted, block someone, or report something from the … button next to it.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for help deleting it, through Support.

Children

FlopShot isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data travels to our servers over encrypted connections, and server-side access rules make sure each person can only read what they’re allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For important changes we’ll also try to let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See Support.
