FlopShot

A golf scorebook for your regular group.

FlopShot keeps score for your round and the side games your group plays: Skins, Nassau, Wolf, Snake, Bingo Bango Bongo, Predictions and more. At the end it shows who owes whom, so settling up takes a minute. It keeps track of the money; it never holds or moves any.

On mapped courses it shows the yardage to the green as you walk up, with a satellite Hole Map for the rest of the hole. Your location never leaves your phone.

Sign in to back up your rounds, score live with friends on their own phones, and post your round to your friends’ feed as you tee off for a little trash talk. Friends can watch along live and make their own picks, and nobody but the players ever sees the money. Keeping score works without an account.
