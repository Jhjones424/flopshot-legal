FlopShot

A golf scorebook for your regular group.

FlopShot keeps score for your round and the side games your group plays: Skins, Nassau, Wolf, Snake, Bingo Bango Bongo and more. At the end it shows who owes whom, so settling up takes a minute. It keeps track of the money; it never holds or moves any.

Sign in to back up your rounds, score live with friends on their own phones, and post a finished round to your friends’ feed for a little trash talk. Keeping score works without an account.

FlopShot is in a private beta. If a friend sent you here, ask them for a TestFlight invitation. TestFlight is Apple’s app for trying apps before they’re in the App Store.
