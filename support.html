Support

Questions, problems or ideas

Contact us

  • Email: flopshot.support@gmail.com. In the app, Settings › Help & legal › Send feedback starts an email with your app version filled in.
  • Beta testers: in TestFlight, open FlopShot and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in the app and share it as feedback. It reaches us with your device details attached.

We aim to reply within a few days. Reports of abusive content made in the app are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile › Settings, scroll to Account and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your profile, Venmo username, friendships and Feed posts are deleted at once. Rounds you played with other account holders stay for them, under your name as it was. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the … button next to the quip or reply, then Report… or Block. Blocking ends the friendship and hides each of you from the other’s posts; they aren’t told. You can unblock in Settings › Privacy › Blocked accounts.

Why can’t I post something?

FlopShot refuses slurs and other banned words in posts, replies, names and handles. Everyday swearing is fine. See the community rules.

Why do I see “s***” in the Feed?

Swearing is hidden by default. To see it as written, turn on Settings › Feed › Allow s*** talk. The same switch also gives FlopShot’s own jokes a saltier voice.

Who can see my rounds and my money?

Players in a round see everything, including the money. Friends can see scores and results (never money) if you allow it in Settings › Privacy. Spectators see the round live, also without money.

How do I watch a friend’s round?

A player can invite you to watch on the “Look good?” screen before they tee off, or from the round’s menu later. The invitation shows on Play Home: tap Watch and pick to make your own picks before the first score, or Just watch. If a friend posts their round to the Feed as they tee off, tap Watch on the card. Finished rounds you watched are in Profile › Rounds you watched; they don’t count in your History or Stats.

How do I post my round as I tee off?

On the “Look good?” screen, turn on Post to Feed above Tee it up, or use Post tee-off… in Score Entry’s ⋯ menu. Friends see “Teeing off”, then the holes played as you go, and your result at the end. Your finish quip replaces your tee-off line.

How do I fix a score after the round?

Open the round and tap Fix a score on the scorecard. Change what you need, check whose money moves, then tap Save. Everyone in the round sees a note saying what changed. If someone already paid, Settle Up shows what’s left or what to pay back.

Why don’t I see yardages?

Yardages and the Hole Map only show on courses that OpenStreetMap has mapped hole by hole; the “Look good?” screen says whether a course has them. Also check that Settings › During a round › Show yardages is on and that FlopShot may use your location (iPhone Settings › FlopShot › Location). Yardages are to the front, center and back of the green, not the pin. Your location is used only on your phone, and the map keeps working without signal once the course has loaded.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

FlopShot can notify you about round invites and friend requests. Choose which in Settings › Notifications. If they’re off in your iPhone’s settings, that section has an Open Settings button. Notifications never mention money.

Does FlopShot handle payments?

No. It works out who owes whom; paying is up to you. Venmo links open the Venmo app.

Do I need an account?

No. Keeping score works without one, and your rounds then stay on your phone. Sign in with Apple or Google to back them up and play with friends.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Score as you play; FlopShot syncs when you’re back online.

Can I get my rounds out?

Yes: Settings › Your data › Export my rounds makes a spreadsheet file you can save or share.

How do I change my avatar?

Open Profile, tap Edit, then Change avatar. Pick one of the drawings, or tap + under Your golfer to build your own: skin tone, face, hair, hat, extras, shirt and background, or Shuffle for a random one. Tap Done to save. Friends see it next to your name. FlopShot never uses photos.
