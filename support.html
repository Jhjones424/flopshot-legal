Support

Questions, problems or ideas

Contact us

  • Beta testers: in TestFlight, open FlopShot and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in the app and share it as feedback. It reaches us with your device details attached.
  • Everyone: open a support request on GitHub (needs a free GitHub account). Don’t include private details there: requests are public.

We aim to reply within a few days. Reports of abusive content made in the app are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile › Settings, scroll to Account and tap Delete account, then confirm. Your profile, Venmo username, friendships and Feed posts are deleted at once. Rounds you played with other account holders stay for them, under your name as it was. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the … button next to the quip or reply, then Report… or Block. Blocking ends the friendship and hides each of you from the other’s posts; they aren’t told. You can unblock in Settings › Privacy › Blocked accounts.

Why can’t I post something?

FlopShot refuses slurs and other banned words in posts, replies, names and handles. Everyday swearing is fine. See the community rules.

Why do I see “s***” in the Feed?

Swearing is hidden by default. To see it as written, turn on Settings › Feed › Allow s*** talk.

Who can see my rounds and my money?

Players in a round see everything, including the money. Friends can see scores and results (never money) if you allow it in Settings › Privacy.

Does FlopShot handle payments?

No. It works out who owes whom; paying is up to you. Venmo links open the Venmo app.

Do I need an account?

No. Keeping score works without one, and your rounds then stay on your phone. Sign in with Apple or Google to back them up and play with friends.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Score as you play; FlopShot syncs when you’re back online.

Can I get my rounds out?

Yes: Settings › Your data › Export my rounds makes a spreadsheet file you can save or share.
